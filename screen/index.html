How it works

1. Open this page on the PC and choose “Set up on this PC”.
2. Create the offer, copy it to the phone (message, notes, or nearby sharing).
3. Open the same page on the phone, choose “Use on phone”, paste the offer and allow microphone access.
4. Copy the phone answer back to the PC and connect.
5. Share screen, record, then stop to save.

The connection code exchange is manual in this static-page version; GitHub Pages alone cannot relay pairing messages.

Prototype · Screen and microphone data are processed in your browser. WebRTC connection can fail on Wi-Fi networks that isolate connected devices.
